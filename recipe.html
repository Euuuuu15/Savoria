Garlic Fried Rice with Egg

A quick breakfast or dinner that takes about 10 minutes.

  • Cooking time: about 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1 person
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice (you have this)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (you have this)
  • 1 egg (you have this)
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil (assumed pantry item)
  • Salt and pepper to taste (assumed pantry item)

Steps

  1. Fry the garlic until light brown.
  2. Add the rice and mix well.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Fry the egg separately and serve on top.

Substitutions

No fresh garlic? Garlic powder can replace it. Start with a small pinch and adjust to taste.
