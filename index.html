Questions

How does Savoria choose recipes?

It compares your ingredients with each recipe’s ingredient list and ranks recipes by how many you already have. It uses predefined recipes and simple matching, not AI.

Are the nutrition values exact?

No. They are estimates for general guidance and are not medical advice.

What if I’m missing an ingredient?

Each result lists what’s missing, and some recipes suggest substitutions.
