About Savoria

Savoria helps students and boarding house residents decide what to cook using the ingredients they already have.

How to use Savoria

  1. Go to Find Recipes and tick the ingredients you have.
  2. Press Find Recipes to see matching meals.
  3. Open a recipe to see its steps and what you still need.

How matching works

Savoria compares your ingredients with each recipe’s ingredient list and ranks recipes by how many you already have. Salt, pepper, cooking oil, and water are assumed. Savoria uses predefined recipes and simple matching, not AI.

About estimates

Nutrition values are estimates for general guidance. They are not medical advice.
